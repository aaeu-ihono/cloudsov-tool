> **EC tender significance:** StackIT winning Lot 2 as a standalone provider — no partners — is the clearest institutional signal in this study. The Commission selected it without OVHcloud's SecNumCloud certification or IONOS's established customer base, validating the thesis argument that StackIT is a "dark horse" now being taken seriously at EU institutional level. Combined with the €11B Lübbenau commitment, StackIT enters the 2026–2030 period as the most capital-backed EU provider in the study with confirmed public-sector contract revenue.

## 11. Summary Assessment for Thesis

| Dimension | Assessment |
|-----------|------------|
| **Revenue data availability** | Very low — no standalone StackIT revenue disclosed. Schwarz Digits (€2.2B FY2025) is the closest proxy but covers multiple business lines. |
| **Investment data availability** | High — €11B commitment is publicly announced and sourced. Group capex (€8.6B–€9.6B) is published annually. |
| **Financial viability risk** | Near-zero — backed by a €185B parent with no debt pressure and explicit multi-billion commitment to cloud infrastructure. |
| **Commercial maturity** | Low — external customer base only since 2022; revenue from external cloud operations not measurable from public data. |
| **Bucket classification** | **C for revenue analysis; A+ for investment/capex analysis.** Treat as a separate category in the thesis: "Corporate-backed dark horse — hyperscaler capital, pre-commercial external revenue." |
| **Thesis implication** | StackIT represents a fundamentally different model to OVHcloud or IONOS. It is not a cloud company trying to scale — it is a €185B conglomerate building hyperscaler infrastructure, with commercial external revenue as a secondary objective. The financial risk profile is inverted. |

## 9. Summary Assessment for Thesis

| Dimension | Assessment |
|-----------|------------|
| **Revenue data availability** | Very low — no standalone revenue disclosed. Only a 2015 historical figure (€40.7M) is confirmed. Bucket C for revenue analysis. |
| **Investment data availability** | Moderate — AION (288K GPU equivalent), OpCore (€2.5B/10yr plan, €440M stake sale), France 2030 (~€150M), iliad €3B commitment are all publicly stated but with varying levels of detail. |
| **Scale** | Unknown from public data. Based on profile (9 DCs, ~600 employees, 4 regions), Scaleway is smaller than OVHcloud but growing rapidly. GPU/AI cloud is its key growth area. |
| **Parent financial capacity** | iliad Group H1 2025 revenue annualised ~€10.2B, EBITDAaL ~€4B. Solid but not at StackIT/Schwarz Group level (€185B). The OpCore €440M partial sale and €2.5B infrastructure plan are the clearest signals of investment commitment. |
| **EU sovereignty position** | One of the strongest in this study — 100% EU-based, French private ownership, no CLOUD Act exposure, Gaia-X founding member, France 2030 participant, AION AI sovereignty initiative. Lacks SecNumCloud certification (unlike OVHcloud). |
| **Bucket reclassification** | Move from Bucket A → **Bucket C** for revenue analysis. Reason: iliad equity is no longer listed; Scaleway revenue is not disclosed in any financial filing. Treat similarly to StackIT — "corporate-backed, revenue not disclosed" — but with a weaker parent (€10B telecom vs €185B retail). |
| **Thesis implication** | Scaleway is the EU's most sovereignty-pure cloud provider by ownership and technology stack, but the least financially transparent. Its AION initiative (288,000 GPU capacity) — if delivered — would make it the only EU provider with hyperscale AI compute capacity. The financial risk is that €3B of investment commitments rests on a €10B telecom parent whose core business is mobile/broadband, not cloud. This is a more fragile backing than StackIT. |

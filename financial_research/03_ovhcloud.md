> **Capex model observation:** OVHcloud's high growth capex (21–22% of revenue) reflects an asset-heavy, vertically-integrated model — the company owns and operates its data centres and servers directly rather than leasing from third parties. This compares to AWS which leases some capacity. OVHcloud's total capex/revenue ratio (33–35%) is significantly higher than the cloud industry average, and represents its main structural constraint vs hyperscalers.

> **Institutional validation:** OVHcloud simultaneously holds IPCEI-CIS participation, an EC sovereign cloud tender win (Lot 1), and SecNumCloud IaaS certification — no other EU provider in this study holds all three. The EC tender win anchors OVHcloud's revenue in EU institutional spending through 2032, providing a de-risked revenue floor alongside its commercial cloud business.
>
> **SecNumCloud ARR as a forward indicator:** €24M in FY2025 (+63% YoY) is still only 2.2% of total revenue, but it is growing at 3× the rate of the wider business and represents the highest-value sovereign revenue (French regulated public-sector workloads). Its trajectory is the clearest financial signal of OVHcloud's ability to convert its sovereignty credentials into premium-priced contracts.

## 12. Summary Assessment for Thesis

| Dimension | Assessment |
|-----------|------------|
| **Revenue data availability** | Excellent — OVHcloud is publicly listed and publishes detailed annual results with segment and geographic breakdowns. Best-in-class disclosure among EU providers in this study. |
| **Scale** | OVHcloud FY2025 revenue (~€1.1B) = ~0.85% of AWS 2025 revenue (~$128.7B). The absolute gap is ~117×. |
| **Growth rate (FY2021–FY2025 CAGR)** | ~13% CAGR — solid growth but decelerating from 18.8% (FY2022) to 9.3% (FY2025) as the company matures. |
| **Investment intensity** | Capex at 33–35% of revenue is among the highest in the cloud industry. This reflects OVHcloud's asset-heavy model but also constrains free cash flow — unlevered FCF was only €57.6M in FY2025. |
| **Profitability** | Strong EBITDA margin (~40%) but persistent net losses until FY2025 (€0.4M net income). The gap between EBITDA and net income is driven by high D&A from infrastructure ownership. |
| **Debt trajectory** | Net debt grew from €667M (FY2024) to €1,103M (FY2025) — 2.7× leverage. This is the main financial risk. If revenue growth slows significantly, debt servicing could become a constraint. |
| **EU sovereignty position** | SecNumCloud certified (ANSSI, France) — the most rigorous EU-national sovereign cloud certification. SecNumCloud ARR (€24M) is still small relative to total revenue but growing at 63% YoY. This is OVHcloud's primary differentiator vs hyperscalers in high-assurance regulated markets. |
| **Thesis implication** | OVHcloud is the most mature and financially transparent EU cloud provider. It demonstrates that a European alternative is viable at the €1B revenue scale — but its ~0.85% of AWS revenue shows the structural scale gap. The key insight is that OVHcloud does not need to compete with AWS on scale: it competes in the European regulated-market segment (SecNumCloud, GDPR-controlled data paths) where AWS cannot operate under CLOUD Act constraints. |
